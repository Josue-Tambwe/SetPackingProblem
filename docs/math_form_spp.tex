Let $n \ge 1$ be the number of activities and $m \ge 1$ the number of resources.
The objective is to maximize the total profit:



\[
\max \sum_{j=1}^{n}  p_{j} \, x_{j}
\]



Subject to:



\[
\sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij} \, x_{j} \leq 1 
\quad \forall i \in \{1,\dots,m\}
\tag{1}
\]


\[
x_{j} \in \{0, 1\}
\quad 
\forall j \in \{1,\dots,n\}
\tag{2}
\]



With:

\begin{itemize}
	\item $x_{j}$ : decision variable which is equal to $1$ when the activity $j$ is selected and is equal to $0$ otherwise, $ \forall j \in \{1,\dots,n\}$.
	
	\item $p_{j}$ : the individual profit of the activity $j$.
	
    \item $a_{ij}$ : is equal to $1$ if the activity  $j$ requires the resource $i$ and is equal to $0$ otherwise.
    
    
\end{itemize}

The constraint (1) ensures that each resource is used at most once by an activity and (2) are the integrality constraints.
